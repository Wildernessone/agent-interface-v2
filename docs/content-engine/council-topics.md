# Council topics — the SEO flywheel surface

These are the questions the autonomous author (`scripts/council-author.mjs`) convenes the
council on. Each becomes a draft at `council_pages` → after James approves → a live, indexable
page at `/council/<slug>` that targets the exact decision/comparison people search for.

**Format:** one question per `-` bullet. Optional `:: topic-tag` suffix sets the `topic` column
(used for grouping/related links later). Headings, blank lines, and `>` notes are ignored.
The author skips any question already drafted or published (dedup by question text), so it's
safe to leave the whole list here and let the daily run work down the fresh ones.

**What makes a good topic:** a real decision or "X vs Y" comparison with genuine search demand and
no single obvious answer — exactly where a panel of five AIs that disagree, rank each other, and
hand down one verdict beats asking a single model. Keep them evergreen; avoid anything that dates
fast (specific prices, "this week", named current events).

> Add freely. Lean into the "second opinion" intent — the questions people actually type into a
> search bar when they're stuck on a choice.
