Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Agent Interface ("the Service," "we," "us") is a multi-agent AI workspace operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME]. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Bring-your-own-key model

The Service uses a bring-your-own-key model. You supply your own API keys for third-party AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI). Your prompts and conversation content are transmitted — through our request proxy — to those providers under your own provider account, and are governed by each provider's terms and privacy policy. We do not use your content to train any models.

2. Information we store

  • Account: email address and authentication identifiers (via Supabase Auth; Google/Apple if you use OAuth).
  • Content: conversations, projects, uploaded/generated files metadata, agent memory, and saved prompts.
  • Settings: preferences and which agents/tools you've enabled.
  • API keys: stored encrypted at rest; decrypted server-side only to forward your requests.
  • Connected storage: if you link Google Drive or Dropbox, we store OAuth tokens to access the folders you authorize.
  • Usage & diagnostics: request counts, token/cost estimates, error events (via Sentry) for reliability and abuse prevention.

3. Sub-processors

We rely on: Supabase (authentication and database), Cloudflare (hosting and request proxy), the AI providers listed above, Sentry (error monitoring), and — where applicable — Google Drive/Dropbox (only if you connect them) and Stripe (if/when you subscribe to a paid plan).

4. How we use information

To operate and secure the Service, route your requests to your chosen providers, store your work so you can return to it, enforce plan limits, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Your rights

You can export all your data and permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Settings → Account. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under the GDPR or CCPA; contact us to exercise them.

6. Data retention

We retain your content until you delete it or close your account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. Backups are purged on a rolling basis.
