Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Agent Interface (agentinterface.app) is a reference site about the agent-interface category — a definitional hub, a tracker of agent protocols, and written guides. It is operated by Wilderness One LLC. There are no accounts. There is nothing to sign into, and nothing you can submit. The site does measure how it is read, with its own records and with Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. This page says what each one collects.

1. What we do not collect

No names, no email addresses, no passwords, no payment details. We do not ask you to create an account because there isn't one. We do not accept uploads, comments, or any other user-submitted content. We do not hold API keys — the earlier version of this site let you supply your own provider keys, and that product was retired in July 2026 along with everything it stored.

2. What we record ourselves

Three records, all about how a page was reached rather than who you are:

  • AI-crawler visits. When a bot from an AI or answer engine (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and similar) fetches a page, we record the bot's name, the path it requested, its user-agent string, and the country reported by our CDN. This tells us whether those engines are reading the site — they crawl before they cite.
  • Arrivals from AI assistants. If you reach a page from a link in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or a similar assistant, we record which assistant it was, the referring address, the page you landed on, and the country reported by our CDN.
  • Page views. On the hub, the tracker and the guides, a small script of ours records the path you viewed, the referring URL, your browser's user-agent string, and two random identifiers described in section 3.

None of these records includes your IP address.

3. Identifiers stored in your browser

Our page-view script generates two random identifiers and keeps them in your browser's own storage, not in cookies:

  • cc_anon_id in localStorage — persists between visits so repeat visits aren't counted as new people. It is a random value; it is not derived from anything about you and is not linked to any identity.
  • cc_session_id in sessionStorage — cleared when you close the tab.

Clearing your browser's site data for this domain erases both, and a fresh random value is generated on your next visit. Browsing in a private window prevents them persisting at all. Google Analytics and Clarity set cookies of their own; those are listed in section 4.

4. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity

Every page on this site except the retired Library notice also loads two analytics tools run by other companies. They start as soon as a page opens. There is no cookie banner and no consent prompt; section 7 explains how to block them.

  • Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-WEF0SF3GYY). It records each page view and, because Google's enhanced measurement is switched on for this site, reaching the bottom of a page, clicks on links that leave the site, file downloads, embedded video plays, site searches and form interactions. Google also adds events of its own, such as a first visit, the start of a session and how long the page was in focus. It sets two cookies on this domain, _ga and _ga_WEF0SF3GYY, which Google lists as lasting two years. Google says Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Google signals, the setting that brings in data Google holds about signed-in Google users, is off, and the account is not linked to Google Ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity. It records how a page is used — mouse movements, clicks, scrolling and what was on screen — and plays that back to us as session recordings and heatmaps. It sets cookies on this domain (_clck and _clsk), and Microsoft sets more on its own clarity.ms domain, including CLID and MUID (Microsoft's cookie list). Microsoft uses your IP address to estimate your location. Clarity masks anything typed into an input box in every setting, and this site has no input boxes.

Like any web request, loading these tools sends your IP address, the page address and your browser's user-agent string to Google and Microsoft. They handle what they collect under their own terms: how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

5. Who processes this

Cloudflare hosts the site and serves every request. Supabase stores the site's own content (the tracker and the guides) and receives the records in section 2. Google and Microsoft run the tools in section 4. The hub, the tracker, the guides and the 404 page also load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser fetches those from Google too. There are no ads and no advertising network on any page.

6. How we use it

Only to understand whether the site is being read and found — which pages get traffic, how people move through them, and whether AI assistants are crawling and citing them. We do not sell this information, and we do not use it to profile or target anyone.

7. Your choices

There is no consent prompt, so these are the ways to opt out:

  • A content or tracker blocker that blocks googletagmanager.com and clarity.ms stops both tools from loading.
  • Google's Analytics opt-out add-on, for Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, tells Google Analytics not to use your visits.
  • Microsoft's Clarity FAQ explains how to opt out of Clarity, and says Clarity honors the Global Privacy Control signal.
  • Turning off JavaScript for this site stops all three page scripts: ours, Google's and Microsoft's. The crawler and AI-assistant records in section 2 are made by our server, so they don't depend on JavaScript.
  • Clearing site data for this domain removes our two identifiers and the cookies Google and Clarity set on this domain.

Because we hold no account and no identity, there is no account to export or delete. If you believe we hold information about you and want it removed, write to us. We will delete it from our own records and use Google's and Microsoft's deletion tools where they allow it. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA; contact us to exercise them.

8. Retention

Our own records in section 2 have no automatic deletion date. They hold no name, email address or IP address. Our Google Analytics account deletes event-level data after two months and user-level data after 14 months; that setting does not apply to Google's aggregated reports. Microsoft keeps Clarity recordings for 30 days, except recordings we mark as favorites and a random sample, which it keeps for up to nine months.

9. Links out

The tracker and guides link to third-party projects, specifications and vendors. Once you follow a link you are on someone else's site under their policy, not ours.
